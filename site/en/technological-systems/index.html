TECHNOLOGICAL SYSTEMS

TECHNOLOGICAL SYSTEMS

AET boasts significant experience in the field of technological systems for railway infrastructure and is particularly specialized in the implementation of integrated systems with civil works, managing the entire project lifecycle.

The main types of systems implemented are:

RAILWAY SYSTEMS:

  • Power supply and electric traction systems
  • Electrical substations and transformer stations
  • Signaling systems

ELECTRICAL, MECHANICAL, AND SPECIAL SYSTEMS:

  • Medium Voltage (MV) Transformer Stations
  • Lighting and Power Systems
  • Continuity and Emergency Systems
  • Ventilation Systems
  • Conditioning Systems
  • Water and fire protection systems
  • Fire detection systems
  • Video surveillance and Closed-Circuit Television (CCTV) systems
  • Telecommunication systems
