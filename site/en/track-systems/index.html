TRACK SYSTEMS

RAILWAY TRACK SYSTEMS

The company offers all track system solutions related to the railway sector and directly manages all phases of the lifecycle of its projects:

AET is currently engaged in the construction of innovative track systems – with and without ballast – that offer high performance in terms of vibration reduction and noise abatement.

Reliability, safety, and innovation are the cornerstones of the company’s philosophy and modus operandi. AET is a qualified and reliable partner, capable of designing and developing innovative, safe, and customized solutions in close collaboration with its clients.

MAIN TECHNOLOGICAL SPECIALIZATIONS IN THE FIELD OF TRACK SYSTEMS:

  • Ballasted track systems with monoblock or biblock concrete sleepers
  • Armamento senza ballast con traverse VSB o con sistema ad attacco diretto su platea in calcestruzzo fissa o flottante
  • Ballastless track systems with VSB sleepers or with direct fixation system on fixed or floating concrete slab
  • Railway lines with both standard and narrow gauge
  • Tramway and metro lines
