Civil works

Civil works

AET operates in the field of civil works for railway infrastructure, residential and industrial buildings.

With a specialized technical team, the company manages the entire study of the executive project plans, ensuring high standards of versatility and precision, essential to meet the clients’ demands.

Thanks to its consolidated know-how and the use of the most modern equipment and methodologies, AET is capable of constructing various types of structures.

  • Civil works for railway track systems (Ducts and slabs)

    Lower Alifana, Piscinola-Miano section

  • Artistic installations in stations

    Scampia Station, Lower Alifana

  • Metal structures for platform lines

    Alifana Railway – Giugliano Station

  • Completion and finishing works of railway stations

    Lower Alifana Railway

  • Construction of industrial and residential buildings

    Construction of a residential building in Milan

  • Renovation and restyling of buildings

    Museum building in Turin

AET – Apparati Elettromeccanici e Telecomunicazioni S.r.l. operates in the railway infrastructure sector and specialises in track systems, technological systems and civil works.
