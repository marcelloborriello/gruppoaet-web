5 – COOKIES

  • What is a cookie? Cookies are small text files downloaded to the User’s device when visiting a website. On subsequent visits, cookies are sent back to the originating website (first-party cookies) or to another site that recognizes them (third-party cookies). They are useful because they allow a website to recognize the User’s device. They serve various purposes, such as enabling efficient navigation between pages, remembering preferences, and improving the browsing experience. They also ensure that online advertisements are more relevant to the user and their interests. Based on function and purpose, cookies can be divided into technical cookies, profiling cookies, and third-party cookies.

  • Types of Cookies Used on This Site:

    • Session Technical Cookies: These are limited to the transmission of session identifiers necessary for safe and efficient site exploration. They do not acquire personal data and are managed electronically.
    • Google Analytics Cookies: This site uses Google Analytics for web analysis. Google Analytics uses cookies to help the website analyze how users interact with the site. Information generated by the cookie about your use of the website (including your IP address) will be transmitted to and stored by Google on servers in the United States. Google uses this information to track and examine website usage, compile reports, and provide other services related to website activity and internet usage.

  • Duration of Cookies: Some cookies (session cookies) remain active only until the browser is closed. Other cookies (persistent cookies) “survive” the browser closure and are available for subsequent visits.

  • Third-Party Cookies List: This site may use third-party cookies, such as Google Analytics, to collect statistical and navigation data.
