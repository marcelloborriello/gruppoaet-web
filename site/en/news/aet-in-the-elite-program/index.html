AET in the ELITE Program

AET JOINS THE ELITE PROGRAM

July 25, 2019

In 2019, AET was selected by the Elite program for its financial results in recent years. ELITE is the international program of Borsa Italiana, launched in 2012 in collaboration with Confindustria, dedicated to the most ambitious companies with a solid business model and a clear growth strategy. ELITE provides access to numerous financing opportunities, enhances the visibility and attractiveness of companies, connects them with potential investors, and supports management in a process of cultural and organizational change. The main objective of the association is to bring more liquidity to companies, especially SMEs, and at the same time promote business culture by introducing them to innovative financial tools for growth and investment.

For more information: https://www.elite-network.com/it/private-companies/programmes
