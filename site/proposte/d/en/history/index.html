HISTORY

COMPANY HISTORY

The AET Group began its activities in 1964 in the field of galvanized steel towers for medium and high voltage and heavy metal structures.

Starting in 1972, activities expanded with the production of electrical and telephone accessories and the development of new production technologies. During these years, the group’s companies established themselves as leaders in the metalworking and electrical component sectors.

In the following decades, thanks to the extensive experience acquired and the desire to diversify business areas, AET also established itself in the railway and renewable energy sectors.

Today, the main specializations concern railway track systems and technological systems, directly managing the phases of design, construction, testing, and commissioning. Thanks to the experience and know-how acquired, the company now boasts a consolidated position in its sector and is engaged in the construction of various railway, metro, and tramway lines for several institutional clients.

AET srl is part of a diversified industrial group operating in multiple sectors: real estate, hospitality and tourism, renewable energy, cultural heritage, and private equity.

  1. 1964

    Ing. Salvatore Paliotto & C.

    Production of galvanized steel towers for medium and high voltage and heavy metal structures, later renamed Steel Beton s.r.l. (1998)

  2. 1972

    A.E.T. Accessori Elettrici e Telefonici s.r.l.

    Expansion of activities with the production of electrical and telephone accessories

  3. 1999

    Acquisition of SUVAL Spa

    Competitor based in Milan

  4. 2002

    Merger of A.E.T and Steel Beton s.r.l.

    creating SAEL s.r.l., later renamed AET s.r.l., which still operates today in the railway infrastructure sector

AET – Apparati Elettromeccanici e Telecomunicazioni S.r.l. operates in the railway infrastructure sector and specialises in track systems, technological systems and civil works.
