SOA CERTIFICATION

Entities executing public works of any kind must be qualified and conduct their activities according to the principles of quality, professionalism, and integrity. The products, processes, services, and quality systems employed by these entities are subject to certification in accordance with current regulations. The qualification system is implemented by private certification bodies specially authorized by the Authority.

SOA Qualification is a certification necessary to prove a company’s ability to compete for and execute public works.
AET holds SOA certification for the following categories:
