AET renews its participation in the Elite program for 2025 as well

February 10, 2025

We are pleased to announce that AET has renewed its participation in the ELITE program for 2025.

ELITE, launched by Borsa Italiana in 2012 and now part of the Euronext Group, is an ecosystem that helps small and medium-sized enterprises grow and access private and public capital markets.

The mission of ELITE is to support companies in their long-term sustainable growth by accelerating access to capital, expertise, and networking. ELITE is, therefore, an international network of outstanding companies with a strong drive for innovation and growth.

Becoming an ELITE company means gaining access to a pan-European network of entrepreneurs, partners, brokers, and successful investors focused on helping the world’s best companies turn their vision into strategic plans and achievable results.

Participation in the ELITE program offers numerous benefits, including:

  • Access to an international network of partners: ELITE provides the opportunity to connect with investors and strategic partners worldwide, opening up new business and growth opportunities.
  • Participation in workshops and training sessions: The program offers a high-level managerial training path on strategy and finance, with contributions from top business schools and academic partners. Through workshops and training sessions, companies can enhance their skills to prepare for new market challenges and establish a common foundation of expertise and knowledge among businesses.
  • Opportunity to obtain ELITE certifications: These certifications serve as a quality recognition for companies that demonstrate high standards in sustainability, strategy and finance, and corporate governance.

Participation in the ELITE program further confirms AET’s commitment to growth and business development, strengthening its position as a leader in its industry.
