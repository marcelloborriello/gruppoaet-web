AET enters the railway signaling sector

AET ENTERS THE RAILWAY SIGNALING SECTOR

October 5, 2019

AET wins its first specialized tender in the railway signaling sector, in a temporary consortium with two renowned industry players: Sirti S.p.A. and Mer Mec S.p.A. The contract includes the design and implementation of the new signaling system for the Flegrean lines (Cumana and Circumflegrea) using ACC Multistation technology and preparation for the train control system (SCMT).
