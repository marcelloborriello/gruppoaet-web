THE GROUP

AREAS OF INTEREST

Throughout its long history, AET, driven by a progressive diversification of activities, has strengthened its market presence by extending its entrepreneurial activities into multiple sectors. Today, the group holds interests in real estate, hotel tourism, renewable energy, cultural heritage, and invests in innovative start-up projects.

REAL ESTATE

The Real Estate sector is one of the group’s major areas of involvement, with investments in various asset classes: industrial, residential, and commercial.

INDUSTRIAL

RESIDENTIAL

HOSPITALITY AND TOURISM

The hotel tourism sector is certainly one of the most attractive on the national scene. The Group embraces this vision through a series of market-oriented accommodations in some of the most iconic Italian cities.

PRIVATE EQUITY

In recent years, the group has initiated a series of investments in innovative start-ups to support their growth and consolidate their market positioning. Diversification across sectors as a risk minimization strategy is a key part of the Vision in this business area. The investment areas include: Food Delivery, Biotechnology, Car Pooling, High-Tech (Virtual Reality) and Online Insurance.

RENEWABLE ENERGY

Sustainability as a core company value is realized through an asset in the renewable energy sector.

CULTURAL HERITAGE

Through the PLART Cultural Foundation, based in the heart of Naples, the group aims to spread scientific and artistic knowledge related to polymeric materials, preserving, studying, and promoting a cultural heritage that tells the story of modernity and projects innovative energy onto contemporary times. In 2020, a new location is planned to open in Turin, one of Italy’s most innovative cities for artistic and cultural offerings.

AET – Apparati Elettromeccanici e Telecomunicazioni S.r.l. operates in the railway infrastructure sector and specialises in track systems, technological systems and civil works.
