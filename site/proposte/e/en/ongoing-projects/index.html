ONGOING PROJECTS

MAJOR PROJECTS UNDER CONSTRUCTION

  • LOWER ALIFANA RAILWAY, PISCINOLA – AVERSA SECTION

    Grantor: EAV s.r.l.

    Type of intervention: Implementation of all technological works of the line in Joint Venture with Hitachi Rail STS (within the consortium company Alifana Due scarl) and execution of track systems, ducts, line power supply, Giugliano electrical substation, Melito station systems, contact line supplies, and artistic installations of Scampia station.

    Start of works: 2004
    Expected completion: 2025

  • LOWER ALIFANA RAILWAY, PISCINOLA – CAPODICHINO AIRPORT SECTION

    Client: EAV s.r.l.

    Type of intervention: Integrated contract for the construction of track systems, ducts, line power supply, contact line supplies, and Secondigliano electrical substation.

    Start of works: 2019
    Expected completion: 2025

  • RENEWAL OF SIGNALING FOR THE FLEGREAN LINES WITH ACC-M SYSTEM

    Client: EAV s.r.l.

    Type of intervention: Modernization of the signaling systems for the Cumana and Circumflegrea lines, including yard works and upgrading of technological facilities.

    Start of activities: 2019
    Expected completion: 2027

  • CIRCUMVESUVIANA RAILWAY – DOUBLE TRACKING OF THE TORRE ANNUNZIATA-CASTELLAMMARE DI STABIA SECTION

    Grantor: EAV s.r.l.

    Type of intervention: Construction of narrow-gauge anti-vibration ballasted track systems, technological systems for the Stabia Scavi parking area, technological systems for Castellammare Centro station, redevelopment of Pioppaino station, completion works for Stabia Scavi and Castellammare Centro stations, and partial construction of civil works along the line.

    Start of works: 2009
    Expected completion: 2026

  • NAPOLI PORTA EST (VOLLA STATION) - AFRAGOLA AV CONNECTION

    Client: EAV s.r.l.

    Type of intervention: Design and construction of the Napoli Porta Est (Volla Station) – Afragola AV connection through the restoration and completion of the old route, including both civil works and systems.

    Start of activities: 2024
    Expected completion: 2027

  • METRO LINE PISCINOLA-CAPODICHINO, PISCINOLA AND GIUGLIANO MAINTENANCE DEPOTS

    Client: EAV s.r.l.

    Type of intervention: Design, construction, and commissioning of all technological and civil works serving the new depots in Giugliano and Piscinola, including connections to the DCO of Giugliano Station for complete remote control of functionalities.

    Start of activities: 2023
    Expected completion: 2026

  • INCREASE IN TRANSPORT CAPACITY OF CIRCUMFLEGREA RAILWAY: OLD AND NEW VOMERO TUNNELS

    Client: EAV s.r.l.

    Type of intervention: Design and construction of the connection on the Montesanto – Piave – Soccavo section of the Circumflegrea line, aimed at completing and modernizing the old and new Vomero tunnels, including both civil works and installations.

    Start of activities: 2024
    Expected completion: 2027

  • NAPLES - SAN GIORGIO LINE CONNECTION - LINE TO SORRENTO

    Client: EAV s.r.l.

    Type of intervention: Design and construction of the connection between the Naples-San Giorgio and Naples-Sorrento lines, including civil works, civil and system installations, and track systems. The route develops respectively at grade, in trench, and in tunnel.

    Start of activities: 2024
    Expected completion: 2026

AET – Apparati Elettromeccanici e Telecomunicazioni S.r.l. operates in the railway infrastructure sector and specialises in track systems, technological systems and civil works.
