MISSION

MISSION

AET has built its long entrepreneurial history on the technical and managerial capabilities of its resources.

Strongly oriented towards the development of sustainable mobility, the company specializes in managing projects aimed at the realization of complex ‘turnkey’ railway infrastructures.

VISION

In a constantly growing sector, the company’s strategic vision views the development of rail transport for high-speed and regional travel as the true opportunity to limit road and air travel. This is also the most effective option for pursuing more sustainable economic and environmental policies aimed at developing a more efficient infrastructure system.

AET – Apparati Elettromeccanici e Telecomunicazioni S.r.l. operates in the railway infrastructure sector and specialises in track systems, technological systems and civil works.
