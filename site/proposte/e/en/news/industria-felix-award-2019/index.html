Industria Felix Award 2019

INDUSTRIA FELIX AWARD 2019

June 26, 2019

AET srl, best female-led company in the Puglia region and best company for Net Profit and EBITDA in the province of Lecce. The recognition is awarded by the Scientific Committee, composed of high-profile economists, entrepreneurs, and managers, through the assignment of the “High Budget Honors,” reserved for companies with legal headquarters in Italy with the best management performance and an indicator of financial reliability for solvency or security.

For more information: https://www.industriafelix.it/le-motivazioni-delle-67-imprese-premiate/
